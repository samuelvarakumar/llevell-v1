LLeveLL 3D LOGO HERO UPDATE
===========================

The hero smart ring has been replaced with a procedural Three.js version of the supplied LLeveLL mark.

Effects included:
- Fourteen rounded 3D signal cells
- Staggered assembly animation on page load
- Ceramic white faces with graphite depth
- Acid-lime illuminated inner layer
- Individual cursor depth response
- Soft automatic floating motion
- Click-triggered logo pulse/scatter
- Scroll-controlled separation, rotation and lift
- Orbiting signal dust particles
- Mobile and reduced-motion optimizations
