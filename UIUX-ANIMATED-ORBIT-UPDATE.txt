Interaction:
- Scrolling rotates the tool orbit.
- Each logo remains upright while orbiting.
- Hover lifts and enlarges a logo.
- Clicking selects the logo, brings it forward, adds a pulse/glow and updates the centre label.
- Clicking the selected logo again deselects it.
