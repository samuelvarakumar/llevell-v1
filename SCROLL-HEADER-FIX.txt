SCROLLING HEADER FIX
====================

The header is now rendered as a fixed overlay from App.jsx.
It does not occupy a separate section or add height above the hero.

Desktop behaviour:
1. While the first hero section is visible, the full header is shown.
2. When the bottom of #overview reaches 104px from the viewport top,
   the full white navigation panel collapses.
3. The logo and black Home square remain, and a white menu square appears.
4. Scrolling back into the hero restores the full header.

Files changed:
- src/App.jsx
- src/sections/Hero.jsx
- src/styles.css
